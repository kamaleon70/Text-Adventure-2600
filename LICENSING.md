## Games and generated ROMs

The editor licence is not intended to force authors to publish their games.
The additional permission in `ROM_OUTPUT_EXCEPTION.md` allows a ROM generated
with Text Adventure 2600 to be freeware, commercial, open-source, or
closed-source. Authors do not have to publish their project configuration,
story, artwork, or ROM source.

The one requirement is a reasonable public credit accompanying the game:

> Created with Text Adventure 2600 by kamaleon70  
> https://kamaleon70.itch.io/text-adventures-2600

The credit may appear in a store page, download page, README, manual, package,
or another place players can reasonably find it. It does not need to appear on
the Atari 2600 screen or inside the ROM.

## Editor redistribution and hosted copies

The ROM exception applies to games, not to copies or modified versions of the
editor. Anyone who redistributes or hosts the editor must follow AGPL-3.0,
retain notices, and provide the corresponding source as required by that
licence.
